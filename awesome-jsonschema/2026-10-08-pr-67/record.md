## What it is, and why it mattered

An addition to the Development Tools section for **greencheck**, a tool that tests whether a
validator, gate or metric can actually tell apart the cases it claims to distinguish: damage an
artefact on purpose, re-run the check, and report which mutations escape. The entry was filed with
a disclosure that the tool was mine and brand new, because a list that quietly accepts its
maintainers' own projects is worth less than one that does not; @jviotti merged it anyway.
